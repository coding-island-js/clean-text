Questions

Does it touch anything except the spacing?

No. It squeezes spaces, replaces tabs, trims line ends and handles blank lines. That is all. It does not rejoin your sentences, strip email headers or rebuild your formatting. If you want that, use the full cleaner instead.

Why does it keep my indentation?

Because indentation at the start of a line is usually deliberate. Spaces between words are the damage. If you do want the indentation gone, there is a checkbox for it under Options.

What are invisible characters?

Zero-width spaces and formatting marks that Word, Google Docs and AI tools leave behind. They look like nothing on screen and then break the paste somewhere else later.

Is it really free?

Yes. Nothing to buy, no account. Your browser does all the work, so the page costs nothing to run and there is nothing to charge you for.
