All the tools

Each one does a single job and says exactly what that job is. None of them asks who you are, and none of them sends your text anywhere. Pick the one that matches what went wrong.

The tools

  • Clean up messy pasted text

    The whole job at once. Strips forwarded headers, quoted replies, sign-offs and invisible characters, rejoins broken sentences, then puts headings, bullets and bold labels back and copies it ready to paste.

  • Remove line breaks

    Text chopped into short lines by an email client, a PDF or a phone. Gives you your paragraphs back, keeps your bullets on their own lines, and repairs words split by a hyphen at the line end.

  • Remove extra spaces

    Double spaces between words, tabs from a spreadsheet, trailing spaces you cannot see, and stacks of blank lines. Every rule is a checkbox, and nothing else is touched.

  • Title case converter

    A headline in the style your editor actually asks for: AP, Chicago, APA or MLA. They disagree with each other, and none of them means a capital on every word.

  • Markdown to plain text

    Hashes, asterisks, backticks and link syntax out. Lists, tables and code blocks intact. For when markdown has to go somewhere that does not render it.

The same promise on every one

Nothing you paste leaves your browser.

There is no server doing the work, no account to make, no advertising and nothing measuring what you type. Open your browser's network tab and watch while you paste. Nothing goes out. Or turn off your wifi and use any of them anyway. They still work.
